Background

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Agricultural Education, Communications & Technology (Agricultural Systems Technology Management) at the University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, working across the Digital Agricultural Science and Technology (DAST) Lab and the Agricultural Robotics Initiative.

My research integrates artificial intelligence, machine learning, remote sensing, and agricultural data systems to support precision agriculture and crop monitoring. A core focus is developing UAV-based workflows for blackberry flowering assessment and yield prediction using YOLO-based object detection and multispectral imagery.

Alongside my research career I have co-founded two AgriTech startups, and in 2017 was selected to participate in Startupbootcamp Fintech Dubai — one of the world's leading accelerator programmes. This entrepreneurial thread runs through everything I do, and is reflected in my Graduate Certificate in Entrepreneurship and the Hodges Entrepreneurial Fellowship at UARK.

Before joining UARK, I spent nearly five years as a Cocoa Management Systems Coordinator at the Ghana Cocoa Board (COCOBOD), where I designed large-scale agricultural data management systems and deployed digital field technologies across the cocoa sector.

I hold an M.Sc. in Agricultural & Environmental Science from Newcastle University (UK) and a B.Sc. in Agriculture from KNUST, Ghana.

Deep Learning & AI

Specialty Crop Phenotyping

Geospatial Analytics
